DOCSIS 4.0 CCAP Core Crash Debugging

Firmware & Systems Validation • Multi-module DOCSIS 4.0 test bed

Intermittent crashes in the CCAP (Converged Cable Access Platform) core during DOCSIS 4.0 testing were blocking large-scale validation. The crashes were unpredictable and occurred at random intervals across a multi-module test bed, making regression testing unreliable.

  • • Led a small team of engineers executing parallel test campaigns to systematically reproduce the crashes across varied load patterns and timing scenarios.
  • • Analyzed CCAP core logs — stack traces, register states, and memory dumps — to pinpoint a timing-sensitive race condition in the scheduler subsystem during high-concurrency resource allocation.
  • • Identified the exact crash location within two weeks, reducing mean time to reproduce from days to hours.
  • • Enabled a targeted firmware fix that restored full test bed stability and established a repeatable debugging framework for future CCAP core investigations.
